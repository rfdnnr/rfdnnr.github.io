### What I wanted

Buying perfume is hard to get right. More than 2,000 launches come out every year, most people don't have the vocabulary to describe what they like, and after the fourth try in a store your nose gives up. With three co-founders, I set out to build an app that helps people find a perfume they won't regret.

### What it cost

We started in July 2025, while I was in college. The first interviews changed the idea: 22 of 30 people had real trouble choosing, but the pain wasn't buying for themselves. It was buying a gift, and regretting the purchase. Then came launch: four App Store rejections, none of them about what the app does.

### What I did

- Ran 56 interviews in two rounds, and let them reshape the product.
- Launched a landing page with a waitlist before the app existed, and automated the welcome emails with Make.
- Designed the AI recommendations (see *AI recommendations*).
- Took the app through App Store and Google Play review, from rejection to approval.
- Worked across product, AI, legal and marketing. I wrote the privacy and terms pages, and built the content engine.

### What I got

- 100+ people on the waitlist before launch.
- Live on iOS and Android since September 2026.
- 500+ AI recommendations in the first two months.

### What I'd do differently

**Charge earlier.** We built the whole product first and left the business model for later, and it still doesn't make money. Next time, I'd get a few people paying, even a small amount, before committing to build everything. Payment is the most honest signal of what people value.
