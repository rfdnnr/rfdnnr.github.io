### What it is

A personal knowledge base that an AI agent maintains for me. Articles, posts, notes, research and project decisions go in once. The agent turns them into linked pages and keeps those pages current, instead of letting notes pile up.

### Why I built it

I used to lose most of what I came across. A good post on X, an article, an idea from a conversation: I'd see it, think it mattered, and forget it a week later. The same happened across my projects, where a decision made in one work session was gone by the next. I didn't want to depend on memory for any of it.

### How it works

- **Three layers:** the original sources, which never change; the pages the agent writes; and the rules it follows to write them.
- **One queue for every project.** Work sessions in any of my projects send their decisions to a shared queue, and the knowledge base absorbs them.
- **Contradictions are flagged, not overwritten.** When a new fact conflicts with an old one, both stay visible until I resolve it.
- **Every change is logged with a date.**

### What I got

- 284 pages and 278 logged entries since July 2026.
- A sponge for everything I would have lost: posts, ideas and decisions now stay.
- Every new work session starts from what is already known, not from zero.
